Accessibility Statement

We want this site to be usable by everyone, including people who browse with a screen reader, a keyboard alone, magnification, or reduced motion.

Standard we aim for

We target WCAG 2.1 Level AA across sandboxdigitallabs.com. We consider the site substantially conformant with that standard as of the review date above. Conformance is a target we test against, not a guarantee that no barrier remains.

What we have done

  • Every page can be reached and operated with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator that meets the 3:1 contrast minimum.
  • A "Skip to content" link is the first focusable element on every page.
  • Body text meets or exceeds a 4.5:1 contrast ratio, and control boundaries and the focus indicator meet 3:1.
  • Form fields have persistent visible labels. Errors are announced on the field itself, described in text, and signalled by more than colour, and focus moves to the first field needing attention.
  • Page structure uses real headings, lists, and landmarks so it can be navigated non-visually.
  • Images that carry meaning have text alternatives; purely decorative graphics are hidden from assistive technology.
  • The site honours the operating system's reduced-motion setting and remains readable in forced-colours and high-contrast modes.
  • Layout reflows to a 320 pixel viewport without horizontal scrolling, and text can be resized to 200% without loss of content.
  • Automated accessibility checks run against every page in our test suite, and we review keyboard and screen-reader behaviour by hand when pages change.

Known limitations

  • The contact form opens a message in your own email application rather than submitting to us directly. If your device has no email application configured, use contact@sandboxdigitallabs.com instead.
  • Screenshots of the PromptCept app have descriptive alternative text, but they show interface detail that alternative text can only summarise.
  • Pages on the Apple App Store and other third-party services we link to are outside our control and not covered by this statement.

Tell us about a barrier

If any part of this site is difficult or impossible for you to use, please tell us — it is the fastest way for us to fix it. Email contact@sandboxdigitallabs.com with the page address and what happened. We aim to acknowledge within 2 business days and will tell you what we can do and by when. If you need information from this site in another format, ask and we will provide it.

How this statement is maintained

We review this statement when the site changes materially and at least once a year. The review date above reflects the most recent check.
