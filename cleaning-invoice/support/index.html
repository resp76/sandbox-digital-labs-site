Quick help

Send an invoice

Tap New invoice, choose a client and add your services, then tap Share PDF to send it by Messages, Mail or AirDrop. The invoice is marked Sent when you share it.

Bill a regular client again

Open their last invoice and tap Invoice again. You get a new invoice with the same client, services and notes, dated today. Check it, then tap Share PDF.

Remind a client who hasn’t paid

When an invoice you’ve shared is past its due date, open it and tap Send reminder. The share sheet opens with the invoice PDF and a short, polite message you can edit before sending.

Record a payment

When the money arrives, open the invoice and tap Mark as paid. Its PDF then shows “Total paid”, so it works as a receipt too.

Frequently asked questions

How many invoices are free?

Your first 3 invoices or estimates are free. Every new invoice or estimate counts, including ones made with Turn into invoice or Invoice again, and deleting one doesn’t give it back. After that, CleanBill Pro, a monthly or yearly subscription, unlocks unlimited invoices and estimates.

What happens to my invoices if I cancel Pro?

Nothing. They stay on your phone. You can still open, edit and share every invoice and estimate you’ve made, and mark invoices paid. Only making new ones needs Pro.

How do I cancel my subscription or ask for a refund?

Apple handles CleanBill Pro subscriptions. To cancel, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose CleanBill. While Pro is active, the Settings tab in CleanBill also has a Manage subscription link. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I subscribed, but CleanBill still shows the free plan

Open the Settings tab in CleanBill and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account you subscribed with. If a family organizer has to approve the purchase (Ask to Buy), Pro turns on as soon as it’s approved.

Does CleanBill take payments from my clients?

No. Clients pay you directly, using the payment instructions on your invoice, such as cash, Zelle or Venmo. CleanBill never takes a cut and never holds your money. When you’re paid, open the invoice and tap Mark as paid.

Where are my invoices stored?

Only on your phone. There’s no account, and nothing is uploaded to Sandbox Digital Labs. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the backup can include a copy, as it does for other apps. Deleting the app deletes your invoices, clients and prices. See the CleanBill Privacy Policy.

Can I change the currency or paper size?

Not yet. Invoices are in US dollars, on US Letter paper.
