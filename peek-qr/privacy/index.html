Peek QR / Privacy

Effective November 5, 2026

Peek QR Privacy Policy

Peek QR scans QR codes and barcodes and checks links for warning signs. It works without an account and without collecting personal information.

What stays on your device

Everything you scan, your scan history, open batches, settings, and the QR codes you create are stored only on your device.

The link safety check runs on your device. Scanned links are not sent to Sandbox Digital Labs or anyone else to be checked.

You can turn history off or clear it in Settings. Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

When data leaves your device

When you choose to. If you open a link, place a call, write an email, or share or export a file, that content goes to the app or service you pick, and that app or service’s own privacy policy applies.

Purchases. Payments are handled by Apple. To unlock Peek QR Pro and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase history and an anonymous ID created by the app. We do not link that ID to your name, email address or Apple ID. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

What we don’t do

No advertising, no ad tracking, no analytics, and no selling or sharing of data. No account or sign-in is required.

Permissions

Camera: used only to scan codes. Camera images are not saved or sent anywhere.

Photos: when you scan from a photo, you choose a single picture with the system picker, and the app has no access to the rest of your library. If you save a code you created, the app asks to add that one image to your photos.

Children

Peek QR is a general utility and is not directed to children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information, Sandbox Digital Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children through Peek QR.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this URL with a new effective date. If analytics, accounts, cloud sync or another data-processing service is added, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated before that release.

Who is responsible for your information

Sandbox Digital Labs LLC, a limited liability company organised and operating in the State of Florida, United States. The company website is covered by the separate Sandbox Digital Labs Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about Peek QR privacy may be sent to contact@sandboxdigitallabs.com.
