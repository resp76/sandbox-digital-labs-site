Privacy & your data
PromptCept is designed to work without an account.

Your prompts stay private

Your prompt names, task descriptions, and generated prompts are stored only on this device. PromptCept does not collect, track, or sell your personal information.

On-device generation

Prompts are assembled locally from built-in templates. Your content is not sent to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, or Sandbox Digital Labs.

Privacy Policy

View the full public privacy policy

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included.
  • PromptCept does not transmit your personal data. Content leaves only when you intentionally copy or open it in a third-party AI service.
  • Deleting the app removes its locally stored data.

Terms of Use

PromptCept provides prompt-writing assistance as-is. Review generated prompts before using them. You remain responsible for the content you submit to third-party AI services and for following those services' terms.
