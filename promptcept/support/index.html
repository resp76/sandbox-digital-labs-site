Quick help

Create a prompt

Use Start with your idea for Quick Start, or choose Add New Prompt for the guided flow. Enter the goal, select an assistant, review the details, then choose Confirm & Generate.

Save and find prompts

Generated prompts are saved locally. Return Home to search the library by name, task, assistant, category, or prompt text. Use the star to favorite a prompt or open it and choose Duplicate Prompt.

Copy & Open

Copy & Open copies the prompt and attempts to open ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok. If the app is unavailable, PromptCept opens that service’s website. You remain in control of pasting or submitting the prompt.

Back up or move your library

Open Settings and choose Export Prompts to download a JSON backup. On another PromptCept installation, choose Import Prompts and select that file. Keep exported files private because they may contain your prompt content.

Delete local data

Open Settings and choose Delete All Saved Prompts. Confirm the warning to permanently remove the local library. You can also remove PromptCept from the device to remove app-local data.

Frequently asked questions

Does PromptCept send my ideas to an AI service?

No. Prompt construction uses local, built-in guidance. Content leaves PromptCept only when you intentionally copy it or open an independent third-party AI service.

Does PromptCept require an account?

No. PromptCept does not require a Sandbox Digital Labs account and does not include advertising, analytics, or user tracking.

Where are my prompts stored?

Prompts are stored in app-local storage on your device. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. An exported JSON backup exists wherever you choose to save or share it.

Can PromptCept recover a deleted prompt?

No. Deletion is permanent unless you previously created a JSON export that contains the prompt.

Why did Copy & Open show a website?

The selected assistant’s app may not be installed or may not accept the app link. PromptCept uses the service’s website as a fallback.

Is PromptCept affiliated with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok?

No. Those products and trademarks belong to their respective owners. PromptCept is an independent prompt-building utility from Sandbox Digital Labs.
