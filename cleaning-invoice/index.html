How to fill it in

What to put on a cleaning invoice

  1. Your business and how to reach you.Your business name, phone and email, and your logo if you have one. Clients trust an invoice that looks like it came from a real business.
  2. The client and the address you cleaned.The service address matters when a client has more than one property, or when a landlord or property manager pays for a rental.
  3. A clear list of what you did.List the main clean, such as a standard, deep or move-out clean, and each extra separately: inside the oven, inside the fridge, windows, laundry. Itemized add-ons avoid “why did it cost more this time?” questions.
  4. How you charge.Use the quantity for hours, rooms or visits, and the rate for your price per hour, room or visit. A flat-rate clean is quantity 1.
  5. The date of the cleaning, the due date and how to pay.Say exactly which payment methods you take. A due date such as “within 7 days” gets you paid sooner than no date at all.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is this cleaning invoice template really free?

Yes. There’s no account, no watermark and no limit on how many invoices you make.

Is my information stored anywhere?

Nothing you type is sent to us or anyone else. The invoice is built in your browser. Your business details, logo and next invoice number are saved in this browser so you don’t have to retype them; client details are not saved. Choose Forget saved details to remove them.

How do I save the invoice as a PDF?

Choose Download PDF. Your browser’s print window opens; pick Save as PDF as the printer. On an iPhone or iPad, tap Print, then the Share button, then Save to Files, or share it straight to Messages or Mail.

Can I add services that aren’t on the list?

Yes. Choose Add custom line and type any service, fee or product. You can also change the name of any service after adding it.

Does it work for commercial and office cleaning?

Yes. Use “Office cleaning” or a custom line, and put the company as the client. Use the quantity for visits or hours.

Should I charge sales tax on cleaning?

It depends on your state and the type of cleaning, so check with your state’s tax agency or an accountant. If you need to charge it, enter the rate and the invoice adds it to the total.
