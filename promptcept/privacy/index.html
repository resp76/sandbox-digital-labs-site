PromptCept / Privacy

Effective July 28, 2026

Privacy Policy

PromptCept is designed to build and store prompts locally, without an account, advertising, analytics, or user tracking.

Summary

Sandbox Digital Labs does not collect, transmit, sell, or share the prompt names, requests, clarifications, generated prompts, favorites, or backups you create in PromptCept. No account is required. No analytics, advertising, or user tracking is included in the current app.

Information we collect

The currently published PromptCept application collects no personal data through analytics, advertising, tracking, account creation, cloud synchronization, or a Sandbox Digital Labs server. The app does not contain a crash-reporting or behavioral-analytics SDK.

On-device prompt generation

PromptCept constructs prompts on your device using built-in templates and the selections and text you provide. PromptCept does not send that content to an AI model or Sandbox Digital Labs server for generation.

Local storage, backup, and deletion

Your prompt library is stored locally in the app’s storage area. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. PromptCept does not provide cloud synchronization.

You may create a JSON export from Settings. That file is generated only at your request and is stored, transferred, or backed up wherever you choose. Anyone with access to an exported file may be able to read its prompt content, so protect it accordingly.

To remove the local prompt library, open Settings and choose Delete All Saved Prompts. You can also uninstall PromptCept to remove app-local data, subject to your operating system’s normal behavior.

Third-party AI services

When you choose Copy & Open, PromptCept copies text to the system clipboard and attempts to open an independent third-party AI app or website. PromptCept does not paste or submit the content automatically. Any content you later provide to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, or another service is governed by that service’s terms and privacy policy.

Company website and support

This policy covers the PromptCept mobile application. If you visit the Sandbox Digital Labs website or email support, your browser, email provider, Netlify, and other infrastructure providers may process ordinary connection or message information under their own policies. Do not send sensitive prompt content in a support request unless it is necessary and you are comfortable sharing it.

Children

PromptCept is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. Because the app does not require an account or collect app-user data, Sandbox Digital Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children through PromptCept.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when PromptCept’s features or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated, and material changes will be published at this URL. If analytics, accounts, cloud sync, billing, or another data-processing SDK is added, this policy and the applicable store disclosures must be revised before release.

Contact

Questions about PromptCept privacy may be sent to contact@sandboxdigitallabs.com.
