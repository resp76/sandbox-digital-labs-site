Before you tap

What Peek QR checks in every link

  1. Look-alike addresses.Names like “paypa1.com” or “arnazon.com” that copy a well-known brand with a swapped letter.
  2. Brand names on someone else’s site.Addresses like “paypal.com.verify-account.io”, where the brand is only a prefix and the real site is something else.
  3. Hidden destinations.Links that disguise where they go, bare IP addresses, and look-alike characters from other alphabets.
  4. Short and insecure links.Short links that hide the final website until you open it, and links without HTTPS.

Link types that can run code or open other apps are blocked outright. The check looks for warning signs in the link itself, on your phone. It can’t promise a website is safe, so stay careful with any page that asks for a password or payment.

Peek QR support

Frequently asked questions

Does Peek QR send my scans anywhere?

No. Scans, history, batches and settings are stored only on your device, and links are checked on your device. Content leaves Peek QR only when you choose to open a link, place a call, write an email, or share or export something.

Does “No warning signs” mean a website is safe?

No. It means Peek QR found none of the tricks it looks for in the address. A site with a normal-looking address can still be harmful, so never enter a password or card details on a page you reached from a code you don’t trust.

Why won’t Peek QR open a link?

Links that can run code or open other apps, such as javascript: links, are blocked. For other links, Peek QR only opens them when you tap Open link or Open anyway.

How do I turn off or clear my history?

Open the Settings tab in Peek QR. Turn off Save scan history to stop saving, or choose Clear history to delete it. Deleting the app also deletes everything it stored.

How do I export a batch?

Batch scanning is part of Peek QR Pro. Choose Batch scan on the Scan tab, scan your codes, then choose Export CSV to share a spreadsheet file. An open batch is saved on your device, so it is still there if the app closes.

Is Peek QR Pro a subscription?

Only if you want it to be. Lifetime is a single payment that unlocks Pro for good, with nothing to renew or cancel. The Yearly and Weekly plans are subscriptions that renew automatically until you cancel.

How do I restore or cancel Peek QR Pro?

To restore a purchase on a new device, open the Settings tab in Peek QR and choose Restore purchases. Lifetime has nothing to cancel. Subscriptions are managed by Apple: cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period by choosing Manage or cancel subscription in Peek QR’s Settings tab, or in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions on your iPhone.
