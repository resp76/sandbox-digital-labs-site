Effective November 25, 2026

CleanBill Privacy Policy

CleanBill makes invoices and estimates for cleaning businesses. It works without an account, and what you enter stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your business details and logo, your clients, your services and prices, and your invoices and estimates are stored only on your device. They are not sent to Sandbox Digital Labs or anyone else.

PDFs of your invoices and estimates are created on your device.

Deleting the app deletes this data from your device. If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, the backup can include a copy, as it does for other apps.

Your clients’ information

The client names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses and notes you enter are stored on your device with the rest of your data. Sandbox Digital Labs never receives them. You decide what to record and who you send invoices and estimates to.

When data leaves your device

When you choose to. If you share or print a PDF, or open a link from the app, that content goes to the app, service or person you pick, and their own privacy policy applies.

Purchases. CleanBill Pro is a subscription sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment, and the app asks Apple whether you have an active subscription. We do not receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Statistics from Apple. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple gives us anonymous usage totals and crash reports. They do not include anything you enter in the app.

What we don’t do

No advertising, no ad tracking, no analytics or tracking code in the app, and no selling or sharing of data. No account or sign-in is required.

Permissions

Your logo: you choose one picture with the system picker, and the app has no access to the rest of your photo library. If your device offers to take a photo instead, only that photo is used. The logo is resized and stored on your device.

CleanBill does not use your location or your contacts.

Children

CleanBill is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information, Sandbox Digital Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children through CleanBill.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this URL with a new effective date. If analytics, accounts, cloud sync or another data-processing service is added, this policy and the app’s store privacy details will be updated before that release.

Who is responsible for your information

Sandbox Digital Labs LLC, a limited liability company organised and operating in the State of Florida, United States. The free cleaning invoice template and the rest of this website are covered by the separate Sandbox Digital Labs Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about CleanBill privacy may be sent to contact@sandboxdigitallabs.com.
